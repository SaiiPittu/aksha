Description

The controller forecasts the next day's crop evapotranspiration from a weather feed and
schedules the pump to cover the evapotranspiration deficit, cancelling the run when forecast
rainfall exceeds a threshold.

Claims

1. A controller configured to forecast crop evapotranspiration from a weather feed and to
schedule a pump to cover the evapotranspiration deficit.

2. The controller of claim 1, wherein a scheduled pump run is cancelled when forecast rainfall
exceeds a threshold.
