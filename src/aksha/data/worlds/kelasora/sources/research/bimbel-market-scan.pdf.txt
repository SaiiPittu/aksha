Tutoring centres in three Indonesian cities, 2026 scan (extract)

We visited 52 after-school tutoring centres in Bandung, Surabaya and Semarang. An estimated 38% of
them already use an online platform for at least one class a week. Owners name parent reports in
Bahasa Indonesia and evening connection quality as the two things they check first. Most pay for
software out of the monthly student fee, so they compare platforms by cost per student.
