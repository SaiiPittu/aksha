# Kelasora: what we are and who we sell to

Kelasora runs live small-group maths and science classes for secondary-school students (SMP and SMA) in Indonesia. We sell to after-school tutoring centres (bimbingan belajar, "bimbel"), which enrol their students and pay us each month. Classes, parent reports and support are in Bahasa Indonesia.

Ratna Wulandari (partnerships) collected the pages, the plan feed, a sales chat and the review export in this folder. A typical partner centre has 60 to 150 students and 4 to 10 tutors.
