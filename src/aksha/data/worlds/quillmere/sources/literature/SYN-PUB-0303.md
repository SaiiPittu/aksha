# Irrigation advisory using forecast rain and temperature-corrected moisture

## Abstract

An irrigation advisory service suppresses an irrigation recommendation when a weather forecast gives a probability of rain above a threshold within the next day. The soil moisture probe carries a thermistor and corrects each reading for soil temperature using a fixed coefficient set at the factory for the probe model.

## Claims

1. A method of advising irrigation, comprising withholding an irrigation recommendation when a forecast probability of rain within 24 hours exceeds a threshold.
2. The method of claim 1, wherein the soil moisture reading is corrected for temperature measured by a thermistor in the probe, using a fixed factory coefficient.
