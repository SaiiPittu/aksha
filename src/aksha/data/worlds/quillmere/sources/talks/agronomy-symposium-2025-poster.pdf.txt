Where the soil stops draining: reading field capacity off a probe's own dry-down curve

L. Moreau-Adeyemi, O. Ferreira-Kwan
Soil Physics Group, Vantry University of Agriculture
Probes kindly lent by Quillmere Soil Systems (quillmere.example)

Background
Field capacity is usually measured from a lab sample. We ask whether an installed
capacitance probe can find it on its own.

Method
After a wetting event we followed the volumetric water content as the profile drained. We took field capacity as the value where the drainage rate first stayed below 0.004 m3/m3 per hour for 6 h.
Twelve plots, four soil textures, autumn 2024.

Results
The plateau value was within 0.015 m3/m3 of the lab field capacity on 10 of 12 plots.
The two misses were clay plots that cracked during the dry spell.

Outlook
A probe could use the plateau to correct its own calibration in the field.
