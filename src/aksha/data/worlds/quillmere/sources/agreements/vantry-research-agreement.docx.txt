SPONSORED RESEARCH AGREEMENT  (Reference SYN-RA-2025-03)

Between Vantry University of Agriculture ("the University") and Quillmere Soil Systems Ltd
("the Company"). Effective date: 1 March 2025. Term: two years.

1. Purpose
1.1 The University's Soil Physics Group, led by Dr. Odalys Ferreira-Kwan (Principal
Investigator), will study in-field calibration of capacitance soil-moisture probes using
the Company's QP-3 probes.

7. Intellectual property
7.1 Each party keeps the intellectual property it owned before the effective date.
7.2 An invention made solely by Company staff belongs to the Company.
7.3 An invention made jointly by University and Company personnel in the course of this agreement shall be owned jointly by the University and the Company.
7.4 The Company must notify the University in writing within 60 days of any patent filing that relates to the research.

9. Publication
9.1 The University may publish the results of the research. It shall give the Company 30 days to review a proposed publication and to ask for a delay of up to 60 more days to protect a patentable invention.
