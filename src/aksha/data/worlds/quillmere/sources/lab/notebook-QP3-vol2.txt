p.41  Wed 14 Jan 2026
Re: AG-1  Field capacity without a lab sample

Problem. Every new site needs a soil sample sent away so we can set the field-capacity
point on the probe's calibration curve. Takes 3 weeks and growers skip it.

Idea (RA, with KOD at the bench). Let the soil tell us. After a big wetting the soil
drains and then levels off; that level-off IS field capacity for that spot.

Method as I would build it:
  (a) Wetting event: raw permittivity at the 10 cm electrode rises by more than 15% within 2 hours.
  (b) Track the dry-down. When the fall in moisture stays below 0.4 % VWC per hour for 6 consecutive hours, take the value at that point as the site's field-capacity anchor.
  (c) Update the site curve by blending: new anchor = 0.8 x old anchor + 0.2 x measured plateau.
      Reject a plateau more than 25% away from the running anchor unless the next wetting event agrees with it.

[margin, added 2 Apr 2026, RA] (b2) Before accepting a plateau, require the 30 cm electrode to show its own rise within 12 hours of the wetting event - this rules out a sprinkler wetting only the probe head.

Tried instead and dropped:
  - Fixed factory curve per soil texture class: dropped, error of 6-9 % VWC on our loam sites.
  - Asking the grower to enter field capacity in the app: dropped, nobody knows the number.

Re: AG-2  Temperature drift

Readings swing with soil temperature through the day even when moisture is flat.
  (a) Read the thermistor already on the probe board at every sample.
  (b) On dry days with no wetting event, fit the site's own temperature coefficient from the
      day-night swing (least squares over 72 hours), instead of the one factory coefficient.
Bench only so far: two weeks in the warm room. No field data yet.
