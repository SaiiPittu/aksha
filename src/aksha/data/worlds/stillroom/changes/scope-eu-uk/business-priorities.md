# Stillroom business priorities, H1 2026

Owner: Daniel Sato, founder
Date: 6 Jan 2026

Ranked, most important first.

1. **Accuracy with still occupants.** Facilities buyers reject sensors that report a room empty while someone is in it. Losing a pilot over "the lights went off while I was sitting" is our most common failure story. Relates to C1.
2. **Battery install with no cabling.** Our sales pitch is a 15 minute install with no electrician. Battery life of two years or more is the line buyers ask about first. Relates to C4.

4. **Installer time.** Fewer site revisits after a bad mount. Useful, but not a reason a buyer chooses us. Relates to C2.
