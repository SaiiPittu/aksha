GLACIVO TRACKPOINT
Cellular temperature tracker for refrigerated vans
Glacivo Inc  |  Datasheet  |  Revision C, 2 July 2026

OVERVIEW
TrackPoint clips to the wall of the cargo box and reports temperature
and door state every five minutes over LTE-M, with Wi-Fi fallback at the
depot. Out-of-band readings raise an SMS alert to the driver and a push
notification to the dispatcher.

KEY FIGURES
Accurate to ±0.1 °C across the full range.
Range: -30 °C to +60 °C (internal sensor); optional external probe to
-80 °C.
Battery: rechargeable, up to six weeks between charges at a five-minute
reporting interval.
Data: CSV export, PDF delivery report, REST API.

MOUNTING
Two-screw bracket or magnetic mount. One tracker per temperature zone.
