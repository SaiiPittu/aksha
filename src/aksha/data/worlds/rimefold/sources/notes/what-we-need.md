# Temperature monitoring: what we need

Rimefold (synthetic) delivers chilled and frozen goods for independent pharmacies and grocers in Ontario and Quebec, Canada. 60 vans, most with two temperature zones.

Must have:

- A record per delivery that a pharmacy customer accepts at the door.
- Pharmacy zone 2 to 8 °C; frozen zone at -18 °C or colder.
- Works in Canada (LTE coverage on our routes, prices we can budget in Canadian dollars).

Nice to have:

- An alert to the driver before a tote goes out of range, not after.
- One dashboard for dispatch.

Not decided: whether we buy loggers per trip or rent trackers per month.
