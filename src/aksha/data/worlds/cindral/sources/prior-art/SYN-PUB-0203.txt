Title: Offering given-up appointment slots to patients on a waitlist
Filing date: 9 November 2022
Priority: SYN-PRI-2022-1109, 9 November 2022
Publication date: 18 May 2024
Status: Application published
Legal status (register extract, 1 Nov 2026): Application withdrawn

Abstract
When a patient gives up an appointment, its slot is offered to the patients on a waitlist in the
order in which they joined it, so the patient who has waited longest is offered it first.
The slot is held for the patient who takes it.

Claims
1. A method comprising, when a patient gives up an appointment, offering its slot first to the patient who has waited longest on a waitlist.
2. The method of claim 1, wherein the slot is held for the patient while they decide.
