Slide 1: Fair waitlists for patients who travel far
INV-9 - Grace Obi and Tomasz Wilk, Cindral - 3 April 2026

Slide 2: The problem
Waitlists offer a freed slot to whoever is first in the queue, so a patient who lives an hour
away is offered 30-minute notice slots they can never take, and drops off the list.

Slide 3: How it works
• Estimate each waitlisted patient's travel time to the clinic from their postcode.
• Offer a freed slot only to patients who can arrive before it starts, given the notice and their travel time.
• Among those, offer it first to the patient who has waited longest.

Slide 4: Settings
• Travel time source: public transport timetable, cached nightly
• Maximum travel time: not decided

Slide 5: Status
A prototype ranks the waitlist in two partner clinics; offers are still sent by hand.
The travel-time study is in travel-times-2026.xlsx.

Slide 6: Alternatives we ruled out
• Offering every slot to everyone at once: ruled out, it creates a race patients far away always lose.
