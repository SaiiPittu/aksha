Idea ref: INV-7
Title: Releasing a slot to the waitlist when a reminder goes unanswered

Problem:
Clinics lose a paid slot every time a patient forgets to come. A reminder alone does not
fill the gap, because by the time the clinic notices, nobody on the waitlist can come at
short notice.

How it would work:
• Send the booking reminder 48 hours before the appointment, asking the patient to reply YES or NO.
• If no reply has arrived 6 hours after the reminder, mark the slot as at risk.
• Offer an at-risk slot to the first patient on the waitlist, holding it for them for 2 hours.
• If the original patient replies YES before the waitlisted patient accepts, cancel the hold and keep the original booking.

Settings agreed:
• Reply window: 6 hours
• Hold length: 2 hours
• Reminder lead time: 48 hours

Options we discussed:
• Double-booking the slot outright: dropped, because clinics hated two patients arriving at once.
• Phoning every patient who has not replied: dropped, it takes too much reception time.

Where it stands:
Tomasz built it against the Cindral sandbox diary, and it was tested on 40 simulated weeks of
bookings before any clinic saw it.

Open questions:
• Why 6 hours and not 4: to be written up.
