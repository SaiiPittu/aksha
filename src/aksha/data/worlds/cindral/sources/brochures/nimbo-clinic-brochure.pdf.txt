Nimbo Clinic by Haldane Health Ltd
Published 4 August 2026

Nimbo Clinic is online booking for physiotherapy practices. Patients book
from your own website, and the diary fills itself while you treat.

Reminders. Every patient gets an SMS reminder 24 hours before the
appointment, and an email reminder when there is no mobile number.

Waitlist. A cancellation list offers a freed slot to waiting patients by
text message, first come first served.

Integrations. Two-way sync with Google Calendar and Outlook.

Pricing. Nimbo Clinic costs £39 per practitioner per month, billed
monthly, with no set-up fee and no minimum term.
