# Design note: drone handover cooler with a docking latch

Author: Mira Okafor, firmware lead, Coldwick
Date: 21 Jun 2026

## Background

Last-mile drones cannot keep a parcel cold while waiting at a rooftop pad.

## How it works

1. Dock the drone cooler onto a rooftop pad with a mechanical latch.
2. Transfer cooling from the pad's chilled plate through the latch contact.
3. Release the latch only when the receiving courier scans the parcel.
