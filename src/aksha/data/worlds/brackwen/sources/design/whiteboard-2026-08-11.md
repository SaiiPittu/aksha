Transcribed from a photo of the meeting-room whiteboard by Dana Velasquez. Nothing below is built.

## What we drew

- W1. Find the tote rim from depth edges in the first image, before any grasp is ranked.
- W2. For each ranked grasp, build a sweep map of where both fingers travel along the approach path and mark every cell that crosses the rim.
- W3. If the sweep crosses the rim, tilt the approach up to 15 degrees away from that wall and test the sweep again; give up on the grasp after three tilts.
