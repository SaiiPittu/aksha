SUBJECT: HOW WE PICK WHERE TO GRAB

Every pick starts from one depth image of the tote. We sample about 400 candidate grasps
over the visible items and rank them. The ranking is a heuristic, not a learned model,
because we need to explain a bad pick to a customer.

  G1  Flatness. Fit a plane to the 12 mm square patch under each fingertip; keep the
      grasp only if the fit error is under 1.5 mm.
  G2  Balance. Score the grasp by its distance to the item's estimated centre of mass,
      the centre of the segmented item's depth points; closer scores higher.
  G3  Clearance. Sweep both fingers down to grasp depth in the depth image; drop the grasp
      if either finger comes within 8 mm of a neighbouring item.
  G4  Fallback. Weight G1 and G2 into one score from 0 to 1; if no grasp scores above
      0.35, switch to the suction cup for that item instead of the fingers.

WHAT WE TRIED FIRST
  Picking the highest point in the tote. Gave the best clearance and the worst balance:
  long items were grabbed at one end and rotated out of the fingers.

NUMBERS
  Patch size ............ 12 mm
  Fit error limit ....... 1.5 mm
  Finger clearance ...... 8 mm
  Suction switch below .. 0.35
