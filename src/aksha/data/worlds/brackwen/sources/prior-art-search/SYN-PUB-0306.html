Abstract

A bin-picking system finds the rim of the container from depth discontinuities in a depth image and crops the image to the container interior before the items are segmented, so that the container walls are never taken for items.

Claims

  1. A method of bin picking, comprising detecting the rim of a container from depth discontinuities in a depth image, and cropping the depth image to the area inside the rim before segmenting the items in the container.
