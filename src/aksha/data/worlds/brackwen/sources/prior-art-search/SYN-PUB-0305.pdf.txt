(54) Gripper finger with acoustic slip sensing

Description

A contact microphone is mounted under the elastomer pad of a gripper finger. The controller
band-pass filters the microphone signal between 2 and 6 kHz and declares a slip when the band
energy exceeds a multiple of the no-motion baseline for a short interval, typically 3 to 10 ms.

On each declared slip the grip force is raised by a fixed increment, for example 10 to 20
percent, until a maximum grip force is reached.

Claims

1. A gripper finger comprising an elastomer pad and a contact microphone mounted under the pad.

2. The finger of claim 1, with a controller that declares a slip when the energy of the
microphone signal between 2 and 6 kHz exceeds a multiple of a no-motion baseline, and on each
slip raises the grip force by an increment up to a maximum.
