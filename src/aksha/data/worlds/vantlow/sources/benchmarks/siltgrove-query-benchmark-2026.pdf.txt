1. Summary

Siltgrove returns query results up to 12x faster than Ferrowind.

We ran 40 search queries against a 10 GB sample of web
server logs on each product, from a single client in the
same region, and report the best of five runs per query.

2. Method

Dataset: 10 GB of synthetic access logs, 31 million lines.
Queries: 40 searches written by the Siltgrove team, mixing
term lookups, regular expressions and group-by counts.
Runs: five per query; the fastest run is reported.
Caching: result caches were left on for both products.

3. Results

The largest gap was on a cached term lookup, where
Siltgrove answered in 40 ms and Ferrowind in 480 ms.
