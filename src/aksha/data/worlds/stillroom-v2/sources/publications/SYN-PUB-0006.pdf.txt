(54) Privacy-preserving occupancy counting across overlapping sensors
(73) Proprietor: Solace Buildings GmbH
(22) Filing date: 2023-01-17        (30) Priority: SYN-PRI-2022-0118, 2022-01-18
(45) Date of grant: 2025-02-11
Status: Granted

Description

Each sensor processes its frames locally and transmits only a count of persons and a count of
persons inside a predefined overlap region. A room controller computes the room count as the
sum of the sensor counts minus the overlap count. Each sensor updates its background only while
no motion has been detected for a holding period.

Claims

1. A method of counting occupants of a room monitored by a first sensor and a second sensor with
an overlap region, comprising: at each sensor, counting persons locally and counting persons
within the overlap region; transmitting only the counts; and computing a room count as the sum
of the counts minus the overlap count, wherein the sensors transmit no image data.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein room counts are aggregated to floor level.
