Generated by `scripts/generate_examples.py` from a real run of `aksha demo` ([src/aksha/interfaces/demo/](../src/aksha/interfaces/demo/), tested by [tests/integration/test_demo.py](../tests/integration/test_demo.py)). ## 1. The venture's first pass

Both agents run on the synthetic stillroom world. The research agent asks which geography to cover; the patent agent asks the inventor one missing detail, then asks which candidate to prepare. C1 (self-calibration) is chosen, its forms and counsel-review packet are filled, and the packet goes to the mock counsel mailbox only after approval A-0001 for patent.packet/C1@v1.

   scripted: approved A-0001 for patent.packet/C1@v1; the mailbox received it once

## 2. New facts arrive

Two things change. A closer prior-art publication appears (a new family). The outside consultant's email that suggested part of C1's mechanism turns out to be dated earlier: 2026-01-05 before, 2025-11-21 after. That date now falls before the consultant's agreement took effect.

```console

   added publications/SYN-PUB-0011.json (a new publication family)
   edited disclosures/consultant-email.eml.txt, disclosures/history.csv (the outside suggestion, now dated earlier)
   edited reviews/reviews.json (one review no output cites; the demo's own edit)
   one command recorded 4 moved records and re-ran 19 recorded searches and listings: 1 found new hits
```

## 3. Only the affected work reopens

Invalidation follows what each output actually read. Everything built on C1's extraction and prior art is rebuilt: the comparison, the recommendation, both forms, the packet and its handoff. C2, C3 and C4 and most research outputs read nothing that moved, so they are kept as they were.

C1's next action moves from `prioritize_counsel_review` to `collect_more_evidence`. The recommendation explains why with the new evidence:

- before: business priority 1; implementation: built_and_tested; F3 not found in any family searched; family SYN-PRI-2021-0412 discloses F1, F5; family SYN-PRI-2022-0118 discloses F2
- after: business priority 1; implementation: built_and_tested; family SYN-PRI-2021-0412 discloses F1, F5; family SYN-PRI-2022-0118 discloses F2; family SYN-PRI-2023-0215 discloses F2, F3
- risks after: own public disclosure DISC-01 on 2026-03-10 (trade-show demo); outside contributor P-05 suggested F2, F3 on 2025-11-21, before the note and before the agreement's effective date

The external contributor (P-05) is flagged for counsel with status `needs_counsel_confirmation`. The question changes with the new date:

- before: "An outside contributor suggested F2, F3 before the internal note. Who are the inventors, and does their agreement affect ownership?"
- after: "An outside contributor suggested F2, F3 before the internal note and before their agreement's effective date. Who are the inventors, and who owns a suggestion made outside the agreement?"

Approval A-0001 named patent.packet/C1@v1 exactly, so it cannot cover the rebuilt patent.packet/C1@v2. The packet's diff review is accepted and a fresh approval, A-0002, is requested and granted for the new version.

## 4. A crash in the middle of the send

A child process sends the new packet and is killed with a real SIGKILL after the mailbox stores it and before the receipt is recorded. On restart, recovery asks the mailbox by idempotency key instead of sending again. The mailbox ends with one delivery per packet version and no duplicate.

```console
4. SIGKILL mid-send, then recovery (n s)
   a child process sent patent.packet/C1@v2; the mailbox stored it, then SIGKILL (exit -9) before the receipt was recorded
   at restart: 1 outbox entry open with no receipt; recovery asked the mailbox by key 1 time, sent 0 times; R-0005 ended complete
   mailbox: 2 deliveries for 2 idempotency keys (patent.packet/C1@v1, patent.packet/C1@v2): each packet version exactly once
```

## What the new evidence did to the shortlist

C1 was the one candidate marked `prioritize_counsel_review`. With a closer reference and an ownership question open, it is now `collect_more_evidence`. The saved selection is rebuilt from the new recommendations and still holds C1, as the person chose (person). Its packet goes to counsel with the contributor and ownership question marked for their confirmation, since inventorship and ownership are counsel's decision, not the agent's.
